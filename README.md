## 🌟 Overview

**FurEver Home** is designed to connect pet parents and lovers with verified care, adoption services, grooming, and veterinary support. Built with an interactive warm sunset theme, fluid layout animations, micro-interactions, and visual storytelling cards.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎨 **Warm Sunset Design System** — Custom color palette featuring rich espresso typography, terracotta accents, glassmorphic card overlays, and subtle dot-grid background textures.
- 🐾 **Interactive Paw & Bone Animations** — Micro-interactions, floating bone accents, and walking paw print trails powered by **Framer Motion**.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive Navbar** — Floating glassmorphic header with smooth-scrolling navigation anchors and dynamic mobile drawer menu.
- 🐶 **Hero Section** — High-impact landing area featuring trust highlights, action CTAs, and interactive stat badges.
- ✂️ **Pet Services Grid** — Interactive cards with category badges and hover elevation effects for grooming, boarding, vet consultations, and training.
- 💡 **About Us & FAQs Accordion** — Visual stat counters, verified caregiver trust badges, and an interactive Q&A accordion.
- 💬 **Loved By Pet Parents** — Testimonials grid featuring verified parent avatars and star rating highlights.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend Framework:** [React.js](https://react.dev/) + [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **Styling & Design System:** [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)
- **Animations:** [Framer Motion](https://www.framer.com/motion/)
- **Icons:** [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/) & [React Icons](https://react-icons.github.io/react-icons/)
